📊 Course Overview

GCSE Latin typically has a single tier (grades 1-9). Assessment includes Language (translation, comprehension, grammar - 50%), Literature (prose & verse set texts - 30%), and Roman Civilisation (20%). These lesson plans cover Edexcel, OCR, and Eduqas specifications.
