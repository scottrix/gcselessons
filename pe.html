🏃 Physical Education

Foundation Higher All Boards

GCSE Physical Education combines theoretical knowledge with practical performance. Students study human anatomy, exercise physiology, movement analysis, training principles, sports psychology, and socio-cultural factors. Practical assessment covers three activities (one team, one individual, one free choice).
