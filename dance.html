📊 Course Overview

GCSE Dance develops dance knowledge, understanding, and skills through performance, choreography, and appreciation. Students perform set phrases, create choreography, and critically appreciate professional works. These lesson plans cover 6 strands with practical technique classes, choreographic workshops, and theory analysis.
