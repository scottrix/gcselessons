🔬 GCSE Combined Science (Trilogy)

Foundation Higher All Boards

Complete lesson plans for GCSE Combined Science Trilogy covering all 77 topics across 24 strands (7 Biology, 10 Chemistry, 7 Physics). Aligned with AQA, Edexcel, OCR and CCEA specifications. Double award qualification.

📊 Course Overview

GCSE Combined Science Trilogy covers Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Students take 6 papers (2 per science) and receive a double grade (e.g. 6-6, 7-7). Foundation tier covers grades 1-1 to 5-5; Higher tier covers grades 4-4 to 9-9. These lesson plans include required practicals for AQA.
