About GCSE Lessons

GCSE Lessons provides detailed, structured lesson plans for parents homeschooling their children through GCSEs. Each lesson is designed as a 50-minute class.

Every lesson plan includes:

  • Clear learning objectives
  • Prerequisites and prior knowledge
  • Materials and equipment needed
  • Step-by-step teaching content
  • Worked examples
  • Differentiation for support and stretch
  • Starter and plenary activities
  • Assessment criteria
  • Homework suggestions
