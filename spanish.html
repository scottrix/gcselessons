📊 Course Overview

GCSE Spanish covers Foundation (grades 1-5) and Higher (grades 4-9) tiers. Assessment includes Listening (25%), Speaking (25%), Reading (25%), and Writing (25%). These lesson plans cover AQA, Edexcel, Eduqas, and CCEA specifications.
