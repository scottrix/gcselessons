📊 Course Overview

GCSE Drama develops theatrical knowledge, understanding, and skills through practical exploration of texts, devising, and live theatre evaluation. Students work as performers, designers, or both. These lesson plans cover 6 strands with practical workshops, practitioner techniques, and performance preparation.
