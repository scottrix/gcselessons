📊 Course Overview

GCSE Electronics (Eduqas) develops understanding of electronic systems through theory and practical work. Students learn to design, build, and test circuits. The course includes required practicals that are assessed in written exams. Foundation tier covers grades 1-5; Higher tier covers grades 4-9. Assessment: two written papers (80%) and practical skills assessed within papers (20%).
