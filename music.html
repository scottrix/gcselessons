📊 Course Overview

GCSE Music develops musical knowledge, understanding, and skills through performing, composing, and appraising. Students study set works and wider listening across four Areas of Study, plus develop composing and performing skills. These lesson plans cover 28 topics with practical workshops, score analysis, and performance preparation.
