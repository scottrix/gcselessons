💼 Business

Foundation Higher All Boards

GCSE Business explores how businesses operate, make decisions, and respond to internal and external influences. Students learn about finance, marketing, operations, human resources, and the wider business environment. Case studies and real-world examples develop analytical and evaluative skills.
