📊 Course Overview

GCSE Statistics develops skills in data collection, processing, representation, and interpretation. It complements GCSE Mathematics and supports subjects like Geography, Psychology, and Science. Foundation tier covers grades 1-5; Higher tier covers grades 4-9. Assessment is typically two written papers.
