BS19: Pricing Strategies

Foundation Higher All Boards

Penetration, skimming, competitive, cost-plus, psychological, and dynamic pricing

Lesson Overview

Estimated Lessons: 2
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the key concepts and principles of pricing strategies
  • Apply knowledge of pricing strategies to real-world engineering contexts
  • Analyse and evaluate pricing strategies scenarios and data
  • Develop practical skills related to pricing strategies

Prerequisites

  • Basic understanding of business concepts
  • Ability to follow safety procedures in practical settings

Materials & Equipment

  • Calculator for numerical calculations
  • Graph paper for drawing diagrams and charts
  • Case study materials (printed or digital)
  • Access to business/economics news sources
  • Spreadsheet software for data analysis (optional)
