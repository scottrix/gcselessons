Lesson 4: Exam Practice: command words &amp; question types

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Extended Answer

Extended question: Full-Mark Response Explain why understanding command words is essential for success in OCR J280 Classical Civilisation (8 marks) <div class="

Understanding command words is essential because they determine the type of response the examiner expects. 'Describe' requires factual detail about what happened, while 'explain' demands reasons and causes. Misreading a command word can lead to a low mark even with strong knowledge. OCR J280 has two papers with different emphases: Paper 1 uses thematic command words, while Paper 2 requires literary analysis. Each paper's command words reflect different skills, so understanding them ensures the candidate applies the correct approach. Ultimately, command words are the key to unlocking what the question demands.

Exam Tips: Circle or underline the command word before writing | For 'how far' questions, plan both sides for balance | Short-answer: one specific point per mark | Extended answers need 3-4 developed paragraphs plus a conclusion | Always use evidence from classical sources, not just general knowledge
Common Errors: ✗ Describing when the question says 'explain why' ✓ Focus on causes and reasons — use 'because', 'due to', 'as a result of' ✗ One-sided answer to a 'how far' question ✓ Present both sides before making your judgement ✗ Writing everything you know regardless of the command word ✓ Tailor your answer to the command word ✗ Ignoring the command word entirely ✓ The command word tells you exactly what the examiner wants
