Extended question: Full-Mark Response How far did the Olympian gods reflect the values and concerns of ancient Greek society? (15 marks) <div class="

The Olympian gods reflected the values and concerns of ancient Greek society to a very great extent, though they also revealed tensions within Greek culture. Zeus embodied the Greek emphasis on authority, order and justice — his role as king of the gods mirrored the hierarchical structure of Greek city-states and the importance of law. Athena represented the Greek ideal that intelligence and strategy were superior to brute force, reflecting the Athenian values of wisdom, debate and skilled craftsmanship. The gods' patronage of specific cities showed the importance of civic identity — Athena for Athens, Hera for Argos. The anthropomorphic nature of the gods meant they experienced love, jealousy, anger and revenge, reflecting Greek understanding of human psychology and emotion. However, the gods also revealed contradictions in Greek values. Ares's unpopularity showed that Greeks disapproved of uncontrolled violence, yet warfare was central to Greek life. Aphrodite's power demonstrated that desire could override reason, challenging Greek ideals of self-control. Exam Tips: When writing about the gods, always link their attributes to their roles | Use specific myths as evidence — don't just state facts, illustrate them with stories | Compare different gods' aspects when relevant — Ares vs Athena on war, for example | Remember that the gods' human-like behaviour is a key feature of Greek religion | The Theogony by Hesiod is the key literary source for the gods' origins and family tree
Common Errors: ✗ Confusing Greek gods with Roman equivalents (e.g., calling Zeus 'Jupiter') ✓ Always use the Greek names for OCR J280: Zeus, not Jupiter; Hera, not Juno; Ares, not Mars ✗ Describing the gods as perfectly moral beings ✓ The Greek gods were anthropomorphic and often immoral — their jealousy, cruelty and affairs are central to understanding them ✗ Saying there were exactly twelve Olympian gods in every source ✓ The list of twelve varied — Hestia sometimes replaced Dionysus, and Demeter and Hermes are sometimes included or excluded ✗ Treating all gods as equally important ✓ Zeus, Athena and Apollo were far more significant in Greek worship and myth than Hestia or Ares
