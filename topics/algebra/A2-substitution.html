A2: Substitution

Foundation Higher All Boards

Substitute numerical values into formulae and expressions, including scientific formulae

📋 Key Concepts

Substitution means replacing letters (variables) with numbers to evaluate an expression or formula.

Steps for Substitution

  1. Write down the expression/formula
  2. Replace each variable with its given value in brackets
  3. Follow BIDMAS/BODMAS order of operations
  4. Calculate the final answer
BIDMAS: Brackets, Indices, Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction

📝 Simple Substitution

Rule: Always put substituted values in brackets, especially negative numbers. Example: x = −2 → (−2)² = 4, but −2² = −4
Example 1

Substitute x = 4 into: 3x + 7

Solution:

3(4) + 7 = 12 + 7 = 19

Example 2

Substitute a = 5, b = 2 into: 2a + 3b

Solution:

2(5) + 3(2) = 10 + 6 = 16

📝 Substitution with Powers

Important: Brackets are crucial with negatives: (−3)² = 9 but −3² = −9
Example 3

Substitute x = −2 into: x² − 4x + 3

Solution:

(−2)² − 4(−2) + 3

= 4 + 8 + 3

= 15

Example 4

Substitute a = 3, b = −2 into: a² + b²

Solution:

3² + (−2)² = 9 + 4 = 13

📝 Substitution into Formulae

Formula: An equation linking variables (e.g., area, volume, speed, physics formulae)
Example 5

Use the formula v = u + at to find v when u = 5, a = 2, t = 4

Solution:

v = 5 + 2 × 4 = 5 + 8 = 13

Example 6

Area of circle: A = πr². Find A when r = 3 (use π = 3.14)

Solution:

A = 3.14 × 3² = 3.14 × 9 = 28.26

Example 7

Kinetic energy: KE = ½mv². Find KE when m = 10 kg, v = 5 m/s

Solution:

KE = ½ × 10 × 5² = 5 × 25 = 125 J

📝 Substitution with Fractions and Roots

Example 8

Substitute x = 16 into: √x + x/4

Solution:

√16 + 16/4 = 4 + 4 = 8

Example 9

Substitute x = ½ into: 2x² − 3x + 1

Solution:

2(½)² − 3(½) + 1 = 2(¼) − 1.5 + 1 = 0.5 − 1.5 + 1 = 0

❓ Practice Questions

Q1: Substitute x = 3 into 2x + 5

Q2: Substitute a = 2, b = -3 into a² + b²

Q3: If v = 10, t = 2, find s = vt + ½at² with a = 4

Q4: Substitute x = -2 into 3x² - 4x + 1

Q5: If P = 2(l + w), find P when l = 7, w = 4

✅ Answers

  1. 2(3) + 5 = 6 + 5 = 11
  2. 2² + (-3)² = 4 + 9 = 13
  3. s = 10×2 + ½×4×2² = 20 + 8 = 28
  4. 3(-2)² - 4(-2) + 1 = 12 + 8 + 1 = 21
  5. P = 2(7 + 4) = 2 × 11 = 22

🎯 Exam Tips

  • Always use brackets when substituting, especially for negative numbers
  • Remember: (−3)² = 9 but −3² = −9
  • Follow BIDMAS: powers before multiplication before addition
  • For formulae, write the formula first, then substitute
  • Check units in physics formulae (m, s, kg, etc.)
  • Use the π button on calculator or 3.14 as instructed

🧠 Problem-Solving Strategies

Problem-Solving

When substituting into complex formulae: write the formula, list the given values, substitute step by step, use brackets for every value. For multi-step problems, work out intermediate results. Always re-read the question to check what is being asked.
Multi-Step Problem

The volume of a cone is V = ⅓πr²h. A cone has radius 6 cm and height 10 cm. (a) Calculate the volume. (b) If the radius doubles but height stays the same, how many times larger is the volume?

Solution: (a) V = ⅓ × π × 6² × 10 = ⅓ × π × 36 × 10 = 120π ≈ 377 cm³. (b) New r = 12. V_new = ⅓π(12)²(10) = ⅓π(144)(10) = 480π. Ratio = 480π/120π = 4 times larger (volume scales with r²).

⚠️ Common Errors

Watch Out!

1. Wrong: -3² = 9 Correct: -3² = -9 (it's -(3²)). Use brackets: (-3)² = 9

2. Wrong: 2x² with x=3 gives (2×3)² = 36 Correct: 2×3² = 2×9 = 18 (powers before multiplication)

3. Wrong: Substituting x = -2 into x² - 5 gives -2² - 5 = -4 - 5 = -9 Correct: (-2)² - 5 = 4 - 5 = -1

4. Wrong: Forgetting to multiply in ½mv² → doing mv²/2 but forgetting the ½ Correct: KE = ½ × m × v² — include the half

✍️ 6-Mark Exam Question

Extended Answer

6 marks: The formula for the period of a pendulum is T = 2π√(L/g). (a) Find T when L = 1.6 m, g = 9.8 m/s² (use π = 3.14). (b) If the length is quadrupled, what happens to T? (c) On the Moon, g = 1.6 m/s². How does the period change for the same length?

(a) T = 2 × 3.14 × √(1.6/9.8) = 6.28 × √0.163 = 6.28 × 0.404 = 2.54 s

(b) T ∝ √L. If L × 4, then T × √4 = 2. Period doubles.

(c) g decreases from 9.8 to 1.6 (factor of 6.125). T ∝ 1/√g. T increases by factor √6.125 ≈ 2.47. Period is about 2.5 times longer on Moon.

Mark scheme: 2 marks for correct substitution and calculation, 2 marks for understanding √L relationship, 2 marks for inverse √g relationship

📊 AO3: Reason & Interpret

Reasoning and Interpretation

A student substitutes x = -3 into 2x² + 5x - 7 and gets -34.

(a) Show the correct working.

(b) Identify the likely error.

(c) For what value of x does 2x² + 5x - 7 = 0?

Answers: (a) 2(-3)² + 5(-3) - 7 = 2(9) - 15 - 7 = 18 - 22 = -4. (b) Student likely did -3² = -9 instead of (-3)² = 9, giving 2(-9) - 15 - 7 = -34. (c) Use quadratic formula: x = [-5 ± √(25 + 56)]/4 = [-5 ± 9]/4 → x = 1 or x = -3.5.
