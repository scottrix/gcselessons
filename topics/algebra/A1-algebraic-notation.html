A1: Algebraic Notation

Foundation Higher All Boards

Use and interpret algebraic notation, including: ab in place of a × b, 3y in place of y + y + y and 3 × y, a² in place of a × a, a³ in place of a × a × a, a/b in place of a ÷ b, coefficients written as fractions rather than decimals, brackets

📋 Key Concepts

Algebraic notation is a shorthand way of writing mathematical relationships using letters (variables) to represent numbers.

Notation Rules

Written FormAlgebraic NotationMeaning
a × babMultiply a and b
3 × y3y3 multiplied by y (coefficient 3)
y + y + y3yy added three times
a × aa²a squared
a × a × aa³a cubed
a ÷ ba/b or ⅓aa divided by b
(x + 2) × 33(x + 2)Multiply everything in bracket by 3

Key Vocabulary

Variable: A letter representing an unknown number (e.g., x, y, a)
Coefficient: The number in front of a variable (e.g., in 5x, 5 is the coefficient)
Term: A single number, variable, or product of numbers and variables (e.g., 5x, 3, -2y²)
Expression: A combination of terms with + or − (e.g., 3x + 5, 2a − 7b)
Equation: An expression with an equals sign (e.g., 3x + 5 = 14)
Formula: An equation showing a relationship between variables (e.g., A = πr²)
Identity: An equation true for all values (e.g., 2(x + 3) ≡ 2x + 6)

📝 Writing Expressions

Rules: Write coefficients before variables (3x not x3). Write variables in alphabetical order (ab not ba). Omit × and ÷ signs. Use index notation for repeated multiplication.
Example 1

Write in algebraic notation:

(a) a × b × c

(b) 5 × y

(c) p × p × p

(d) m ÷ 4

Solution:

(a) abc

(b) 5y

(c) p³

(d) m/4 or ¼m

Example 2

Write as algebraic expressions:

(a) Add 4 to x

(b) Subtract 3 from 2y

(c) Multiply a by 7 and add 5

(d) Divide x by 2 then subtract 1

Solution:

(a) x + 4

(b) 2y − 3

(c) 7a + 5

(d) x/2 − 1 or ½x − 1

📝 Interpreting Notation

Remember: 2x means 2 × x. x² means x × x. (2x)² means (2x) × (2x) = 4x². These are different!
Example 3

If x = 3, find the value of:

(a) 2x

(b) x²

(c) (2x)²

(d) 2x²

Solution:

(a) 2 × 3 = 6

(b) 3 × 3 = 9

(c) (2 × 3)² = 6² = 36

(d) 2 × 3² = 2 × 9 = 18

📝 Formulas and Identities

Formula: A rule linking variables (e.g., area of rectangle: A = lw)
Identity: True for ALL values, shown with ≡ (e.g., 2(x + 1) ≡ 2x + 2)
Example 4

Identify each as expression, equation, formula, or identity:

(a) 3x + 7

(b) 3x + 7 = 16

(c) P = 2(l + w)

(d) 4(x − 2) ≡ 4x − 8

Solution:

(a) Expression

(b) Equation

(c) Formula

(d) Identity

❓ Practice Questions

Q1: What is the coefficient in 5x?

Q2: Write 'multiply a by b' in algebraic notation.

Q3: Write 'divide x by 3' in algebraic notation.

Q4: What does 2(x + 3) mean?

Q5: Simplify: a + a + a

✅ Answers

  1. 5
  2. ab
  3. x/3 or ⅓x
  4. Multiply 2 by everything inside the bracket: 2x + 6
  5. 3a

🎯 Exam Tips

  • Always write the coefficient before the variable: 5x not x5
  • Write variables in alphabetical order: ab not ba
  • Don't write × or ÷ signs in algebra
  • Use powers for repeated multiplication: a × a = a²
  • Brackets mean multiply everything inside: 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6
  • Distinguish: 2x² = 2 × x² but (2x)² = 4x²

🧠 Problem-Solving Strategies

Problem-Solving

When translating words to algebra: identify the operations (add, subtract, multiply, divide), identify the unknown (variable), build the expression step by step. For "5 more than twice a number": twice a number = 2x, 5 more = 2x + 5. Always check by substituting a number.
Multi-Step Problem

A rectangle has length 3 cm more than its width. Write an expression for the perimeter in terms of the width w.

Solution: Width = w, Length = w + 3. Perimeter = 2(length + width) = 2(w + 3 + w) = 2(2w + 3) = 4w + 6.

⚠️ Common Errors

Watch Out!

1. Wrong: x3 means 3x Correct: Always write coefficient first: 3x

2. Wrong: (2x)² = 2x² Correct: (2x)² = (2x)(2x) = 4x²

3. Wrong: x/2 means 2/x Correct: x/2 means x divided by 2 (x ÷ 2)

4. Wrong: a + b = ab Correct: a + b cannot be simplified; ab means a × b

✍️ 6-Mark Exam Question

Extended Answer

6 marks: A taxi firm charges £3 fixed fee plus £2 per mile. (a) Write a formula for the total cost C for m miles. (b) Find the cost for 5 miles. (c) A journey costs £19. How many miles?

(a) C = 3 + 2m (or C = 2m + 3)

(b) C = 3 + 2×5 = 3 + 10 = £13

(c) 19 = 3 + 2m → 16 = 2m → m = 8 miles

Mark scheme: 2 marks for correct formula, 2 marks for correct substitution and answer, 2 marks for solving equation with working

📊 AO3: Reason & Interpret

Reasoning and Interpretation

Two students write expressions for "subtract 4 from 3 times a number".

Student A writes: 3x − 4

Student B writes: 4 − 3x

(a) Which student is correct? Explain.

(b) For what value of x would both expressions give the same result?

(c) Write an expression for "4 less than 3 times a number" — is it the same or different?

Answers: (a) Student A. "Subtract 4 from 3x" means start with 3x, then take away 4 → 3x − 4. (b) 3x − 4 = 4 − 3x → 6x = 8 → x = 4/3. (c) "4 less than 3x" also means 3x − 4. Same meaning.
