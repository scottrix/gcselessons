Lesson 2: Core Concepts: gradients & intercepts

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on gradients & intercepts. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Gradient: The steepness of a line. Found by dividing vertical change by horizontal change.
Intercepts: y-intercept: Where the line crosses the y-axis (when x = 0) x-intercept: Where the line crosses the x-axis (when y = 0)

Practice (10 minutes)

Q: Find the gradient of the line passing through (0, 2) and (4, 10).

Answer: 2

Plenary (5 minutes)

Explain Back

Your student teaches the key points back to you without looking. Fill any gaps immediately.

Lesson 3: Application: gradients & intercepts

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Quick Recall

Recall the key terms: Gradient, Intercepts. Define each in one sentence.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Parent/Teacher Guide: Let your student attempt each question alone first, then compare with the model answer. Award method marks for correct working even if the final answer is wrong.

Q1: Find the gradient of the line passing through (0, 2) and (4, 10).

Answer: 2

Q2: Find the y-intercept of y = 5x - 8.

Answer: (0, -8)

Q3: Find the x-intercept of y = 3x + 9.

Answer: (-3, 0)

Q4: A line has gradient -2 and passes through (1, 5). Find its y-intercept.

Answer: (0, 7)

Q5: A distance-time graph shows d = 50t + 100. What does the gradient represent?

Answer: Speed = 50 (units per hour)

Q6: Write the equation of a line with gradient 4 and y-intercept (0, -1).

Answer: y = 4x - 1

Error Review

Review any questions answered incorrectly. Identify whether the error was knowledge, method, or reading the question.

Lesson 4: Exam Practice: gradients & intercepts

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Extended Answer

Extended question: Extended Answer 6 marks: Two water tanks are being drained. Tank A: d = 120 - 8t. Tank B: d = 100 - 5t. d = depth in cm, t = time in minutes. (a) Which tank starts fuller? (b) Which drains faster? (c) After how many minutes is Tank A empty? (d) Which tank still has water left after 15 minutes? <div class="

(a) Tank A starts at 120 cm, Tank B at 100 cm. Tank A is fuller. (b) Tank A drains at 8 cm/min (gradient = -8). Tank B drains at 5 cm/min. Tank A drains faster. (c) 120 - 8t = 0 → t = 15 minutes (d) After 15 minutes: Tank A = 120 - 8(15) = 0 cm (empty). Tank B = 100 - 5(15) = 25 cm. Tank B still has water. Mark scheme: (a) 1 mark. (b) 1 mark with reasoning. (c) 2 marks. (d) 2 marks for both tanks checked.

Exam Tips: y-intercept: substitute x = 0 into the equation | x-intercept: substitute y = 0 and solve for x | Gradient = change in y ÷ change in x | Positive gradient = uphill; negative = downhill | In context, gradient often means "rate" (price per unit, speed, etc.)
Common Errors: Watch Out! 1. Wrong: The y-intercept of y = 5x - 3 is 3 Correct: The y-intercept is -3 (the sign is included: y-intercept at (0, -3)) 2. Wrong: Gradient = horizontal change ⁄ vertical change Correct: Gradient = vertical change ⁄ horizontal change = Δy ⁄ Δx 3. Wrong: In C = 30t + 50, the gradient means "30 hours per £" Correct: The gradient 30 means £30 per hour (units of y per unit of x)
AO3 - Reasoning & Interpretation: Reasoning and Interpretation A monthly phone bill is B = 0.05t + 12, where B = cost (£), t = number of texts. (a) Explain what the gradient and y-intercept represent. (b) If the bill is £27, how many texts were sent? (c) A rival network charges B = 0.08t + 8. After how many texts is it cheaper to stay with the original network? Answers: (a) Gradient 0.05 = 5p per text. y-intercept 12 = £12 fixed monthly charge. (b) 27 = 0.05t + 12 → 0.05t = 15 → t = 300 texts. (c) 0.05t + 12 133.3. Original network cheaper after 134+ texts.
