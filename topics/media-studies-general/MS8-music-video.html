Lesson 2: Core Concepts: music video

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on music video. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Key Fact: Music videos serve multiple purposes: promoting the song and artist, extending the artist's star image, creating visual narratives, and generating additional revenue and audience engagement.
Key Fact: Performance videos feature the artist performing (singing, dancing, playing instruments), creating a sense of authenticity and connecting the audience with the artist's live persona.
Key Fact: Narrative videos tell a story — either illustrating the song's lyrics (amplification) or creating a separate narrative. Some combine performance and narrative elements.
Key Fact: Conventions of music video: quick cuts and editing to the beat, close-ups of the artist, stylised lighting and colour, choreography, and visual motifs linked to the song.
Key Fact: Star image (Dyer): the music video constructs and maintains the artist's star persona. Stars are not real people — their image is carefully crafted through visual codes and narratives.
Key Fact: Andrew Goodwin identified six key features of music videos: genre characteristics, relationship between lyrics and visuals, relationship between music and visuals, demands of the record label (close-ups), frequent reference to notion of looking, and intertextual reference.

Extended question: Full-Mark Response Analyse how one of your music video CSPs constructs the artist's star image. [12 marks] <div class="

Music videos are a primary site for the construction and maintenance of star image, using visual, audio, and narrative codes to create a persona that audiences connect with. Dyer argues that stars are constructed images, not real people. In music videos, this construction happens through deliberate choices of costume, setting, performance style, and narrative positioning. The artist's star image must be both familiar enough to be recognisable and fresh enough to maintain audience interest. Goodwin's features help analyse this construction. Record labels demand frequent close-ups of the artist because the star image is the primary selling point — audiences connect with the face and persona, not just the music. Performance videos create authenticity (the artist as musician), while narrative videos add depth (the artist as storyteller or character). The relationship between lyrics and visuals is crucial for star image. Amplification — where the video expands on the song's themes rather than simply illustrating them — creates a richer star persona. This shows the artist as creative and thoughtful, adding cultural value. Intertextual references position the artist within a cultural line

Exam Tips: Apply Goodwin's features to specific music video CSPs by name | Discuss how the video constructs the artist's star image | Consider the relationship between visuals and lyrics/music
