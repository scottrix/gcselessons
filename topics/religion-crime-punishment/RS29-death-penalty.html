RS29: Death Penalty

Foundation Higher All Boards

Detailed lesson plans for death penalty in GCSE Religious Studies.

Lesson Overview

Estimated Lessons: 3
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, Eduqas, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Understand key concepts in death penalty
  • Apply knowledge to exam-style questions
  • Develop subject-specific skills

Prerequisites

  • Basic understanding of religion crime punishment
  • Familiarity with key terminology

Materials & Equipment

  • Exercise book and pen
  • Scientific calculator (if applicable)
  • Religious texts (extracts provided)
