GM7-family-celebrations: Family Celebrations

Foundation Higher All Boards

Family/event vocab, future tense (werden), reflexive verbs, writing invitations

Lesson Overview

Lesson: 3 of 4
Focus: Structured production building to extended writing/speaking
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, Eduqas, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Master key vocabulary for family celebrations
  • Understand and apply structured production building to extended writing/speaking
  • Develop vocabulary, grammar, reading skills in context
  • Build confidence with exam-style tasks

Starter (5 min)

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter (5 min) (5 min)

Activity

Grammar quick-fire: conjugate verb / decline noun / translate phrase on mini-whiteboards.

Main (35 min)

Duration: 50 minutes

Main (35 min) (35 min)

Activity

Writing: model text analysis (structure, connectives, tenses). Scaffolded writing frame: paragraph 1 (intro), 2 (detail), 3 (opinion), 4 (future). Students write 90-150 words. Speaking: photo card / role-play / general conversation practice. Peer assessment with mark scheme.

Plenary (10 min)

Duration: 50 minutes

Plenary (10 min) (10 min)

Activity

WWW/EBI: students swap work, give 2 strengths, 1 improvement using criteria.

Core Vocabulary (20-25 words): Topic-specific nouns, verbs, adjectives, connectives, time expressions, opinion phrases.

Homework Suggestions

  • Polish writing piece for submission. Record 1-min speaking response on phone. Learn key phrases for speaking.
