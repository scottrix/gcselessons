AD4: Presenting Personal Outcomes

Foundation Higher All Boards

Bring your investigation to a resolved conclusion. Create ambitious final outcome(s) that answer your enquiry, and present them professionally for assessment. Learn installation, documentation, and evaluation skills.

Lesson Overview

Estimated Lessons: 5
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 60 minutes per lesson
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, CCEA, WJEC
Practical: Yes

Learning Objectives

  • Plan and execute resolved final outcome(s) from developed investigations
  • Make informed decisions about scale, media, presentation format
  • Install/present work with consideration of viewer experience
  • Document outcomes professionally for submission
  • Write evaluative commentary linking outcome to enquiry journey

Prerequisites

  • Completed enquiry with substantial development work (AD1-AD3)
  • Clear enquiry question and success criteria
  • Technical competence in chosen media

Materials & Equipment

  • Production: All media from AD1, scaled up (larger paper, canvas, board), specialist materials per outcome (resin, fabric, timber, etc.)
  • Presentation: Mount board, frames, pins, Velcro, plinths, shelves, hanging system, lighting (clip lamps), label cards
  • Documentation: DSLR/phone, tripod, lighting kit, grey card, colour checker, laptop for editing
  • Installation Space: Wall space (min 2m per student), floor space for 3D, good lighting, clean walls, step ladder
  • Safety: Heavy lifting guidance, ladder safety, electrical safety for lighting, chemical safety for resins/solvents, fire exits clear
  • Time: Extended sessions may be needed for large-scale work — plan for 2-3 hour blocks

Lesson 1: Outcome Planning & Proposal

Duration: 60 minutes

Warm-up (10 minutes)

Outcome Speed-Sketches

5 minutes: sketch 3 different final outcome possibilities for your enquiry. Different media, scale, format. Annotate each: "This would show... because..."

Skill Development (35 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
From enquiry to outcome:
  • Outcome Types: Single resolved piece, series (3-5 related), installation, book/zine, digital projection, performance documentation, garment, furniture, architectural model.
  • Decision Framework: What does the enquiry DEMAND? (Not what do I like doing). Scale: intimate vs immersive. Media: best serves concept? Format: wall, floor, screen, body, page?
  • Project Plan: Timeline (Gantt chart), materials list with costs/sources, technical tests needed, contingency time (20%), installation plan.
  • Risk Assessment: Materials, tools, space, time. Mitigation for each risk.

Required Practical: Outcome Proposal Document

A3 sheet: Enquiry question (refined), Outcome description (100 words), Media & scale justification, Timeline (week-by-week), Materials budget, Technical tests planned (min 3), Installation sketch with measurements, Risk assessment table. Teacher sign-off before production.

Common Misconception

"The final outcome is the most important part"

The JOURNEY (development, experimentation, refinement) carries equal or more weight. A resolved outcome without evidence of struggle, decision-making, and development is suspect.

Differentiation

Support: Provide outcome proposal template with prompts. Suggest 2-3 viable outcome routes. Break timeline into daily tasks.
Stretch: Propose ambitious multi-part installation. Include interactive/digital element. Write artist statement draft. Plan public exhibition context.

Creative Work (10 minutes)

Material Test

Execute ONE critical material test from proposal (e.g., resin pour on test piece, fabric drape test, large-scale charcoal gesture). Document results.

Reflection (5 minutes)

Peer Proposal Review

Swap proposals. Check: Does outcome answer the enquiry? Is timeline realistic? Are tests sufficient? Any safety gaps? Feedback on sticky notes.

Assessment Criteria

  • Technical Skill: Realistic planning, appropriate media choices
  • Creativity: Ambitious but achievable, innovative format consideration
  • Understanding: Clear enquiry-outcome link, thorough risk assessment
  • Presentation: Professional proposal document, clear visuals

Lesson 2: Production Phase 1 - Foundations

Duration: 60 minutes

Warm-up (5 minutes)

Studio Setup

Prepare workspace: cover surfaces, organise materials, set up lighting, queue reference images, fill water pots, test equipment.

Skill Development (15 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
Production management:
  • Groundwork: Stretch canvas, prime boards, cut paper to size, build armatures, prepare screens/blocks.
  • Underpainting/Base Layers: Establish composition, tonal structure, colour foundation. Work large → small, loose → tight.
  • Documentation Habit: Photograph at start, end of each session. Date-stamped. Creates process evidence automatically.
  • Pacing: Aim for 70% complete by end of Lesson 3. Final 30% = refinement, not major changes.

Required Practical: Production Start

Begin final outcome production. Minimum: groundwork complete, underpainting/base layer established, composition resolved at full scale. Photograph start and end of session. Log: time, media, decisions, issues.

Creative Work (35 minutes)

Focused Production

Uninterrupted studio time. Teacher circulates for 1:1 technical support. Students work to their individual plans.

Reflection (5 minutes)

Session Log

Quick note: What progressed? What stalled? Next session priority. Adjust timeline if needed.

Assessment Criteria

  • Technical Skill: Competent groundwork, confident media handling at scale
  • Creativity: Composition works at final scale, media serving concept
  • Understanding: Adhering to plan or adapting with reason, documentation habit
  • Presentation: Clean workspace, organised materials, labelled work-in-progress

Lesson 3: Production Phase 2 - Development

Duration: 60 minutes

Warm-up (5 minutes)

Fresh Eyes

Step back 3m. View work upside down. Take photo, view on phone screen. What jumps out? Note 3 adjustments needed.

Skill Development (10 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
Mid-production review:
  • Critical Checkpoints: Composition balance, focal points, tonal range, colour harmony, surface quality, concept legibility.
  • Common Issues: Overworked areas, lost contrast, muddy colour, scale discrepancies, technical failures (cracking, warping).
  • Intervention Strategies: Glazing to unify, scraping back, cropping (digital or physical), adding accent, changing ground.

Required Practical: Mid-Production Review & Continue

Formal review: photograph work, annotate 3 strengths, 3 areas for development, specific actions for this session. Continue production targeting those areas. Photograph end of session.

Creative Work (40 minutes)

Targeted Development

Work on identified areas. Teacher provides technical demos as needed (glazing, blending, structural reinforcement, digital correction).

Reflection (5 minutes)

Progress Rating

Rate progress 1-10. If <7, what specific help/time needed? Communicate to teacher.

Assessment Criteria

  • Technical Skill: Problem-solving, technique refinement, control
  • Creativity: Responding to work's needs, not forcing preconceived image
  • Understanding: Articulate mid-process analysis, evidence-based adjustments
  • Presentation: Work protected/stored correctly, documentation current

Lesson 4: Production Phase 3 - Resolution

Duration: 60 minutes

Warm-up (5 minutes)

Final Push Plan

List exactly 5 actions to complete the outcome. Prioritise. Share with peer for accountability.

Skill Development (10 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
Resolution strategies:
  • Knowing It's Done: Returns to enquiry question — does it answer? No more "just one more thing."
  • Final Touches: Edges, surfaces, signatures, fixative, varnish (test first!), mounting preparation.
  • Multi-Piece Consistency: If series — shared palette, scale relationships, mounting unity, narrative flow.
  • 3D/Specific: Structural integrity, finish quality, base/plinth, lighting consideration, stability.

Required Practical: Outcome Completion

Complete final outcome(s) to resolved standard. Apply final touches. Sign/date. Photograph final state in situ. Create "finished" checklist signed by student and teacher.

Creative Work (40 minutes)

Resolution Sprint

Focused completion. Teacher available for final technical decisions. Quiet studio atmosphere.

Reflection (5 minutes)

Completion Declaration

"This work is complete because..." written on back of work/in digital metadata.

Assessment Criteria

  • Technical Skill: Resolved finish, appropriate craftsmanship, media mastery
  • Creativity: Outcome authentically answers enquiry, shows personal voice
  • Understanding: Clear resolution decision, links back to journey
  • Presentation: Work ready for installation, documented, protected

Lesson 5: Installation, Documentation & Evaluation

Duration: 60 minutes

Warm-up (10 minutes)

Installation Walk-Through

Students install work in allocated space. 10 min each. Peers observe: sightlines, lighting, spacing, labels. Quick feedback.

Skill Development (20 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
Professional presentation:
  • Installation: Height (centre 150cm), spacing, lighting angle, labels (title, media, dimensions), viewer flow.
  • Photography: Overall installation shot, individual work shots, detail shots, context shots (viewer perspective). Raw + edited.
  • Digital Submission: File prep per spec. Metadata. Backup.

Required Practical: Final Documentation & Evaluation

1. Install & photograph outcome(s) professionally. 2. Write 300-word evaluation: Enquiry question → Journey summary → Outcome analysis (successes, limitations) → What would you develop further? 3. Final portfolio check. 4. Backup all files.

Creative Work (20 minutes)

Installation & Shoot

Students finalise installation, complete photography. Teacher supports lighting/composition.

Reflection (10 minutes)

Course Reflection

Whole group: "One thing I learned about my practice," "One risk that paid off," "Advice for next year's students."

Assessment Criteria

  • Technical Skill: Professional documentation, installation competence
  • Creativity: Considered presentation enhancing work's meaning
  • Understanding: Insightful evaluation, honest self-assessment, forward-looking
  • Presentation: Submission-ready files, complete portfolio, evaluation included

Homework Suggestions

  • Complete evaluation writing if not finished in class
  • Final file organisation and backup verification
  • Mock moderation: Present portfolio to someone unfamiliar with your work
  • Create exhibition invitation/catalogue page for your outcome (portfolio enhancement)
  • Rest. Celebrate.
