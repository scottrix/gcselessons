Required Practical: The Milky Way

AQA Spec Ref: RP-15 | Edexcel Spec Ref: CP-15

Method

  1. Set up apparatus as shown in the diagram
  2. Take initial readings and record in a table
  3. Carry out the procedure, recording observations at regular intervals
  4. Process data: calculate means, plot graphs, determine gradients
  5. Evaluate: identify sources of error, suggest improvements

Safety

  • Wear eye protection throughout
  • Handle equipment with care
  • Follow CLEAPSS guidelines for any hazardous materials

Data Analysis

Record results in a table with appropriate headings and units. Plot a graph of [dependent variable] against [independent variable]. Calculate the gradient and use it to determine [physical constant]. Compare your result to the accepted value and calculate percentage error.
