Extended question: Full-Mark Response How should you approach a comparative question about two global films in Component 2? <div class="

A comparative question in Component 2 requires a sustained, balanced analysis of both set films that identifies meaningful similarities and differences and explains their significance. Begin by reading the question carefully and identifying the specific aspect you are asked to compare, such as representation, theme or the use of film language. Establish a comparative thesis in your introduction that states your key argument about how the films relate on this aspect. Use point-by-point comparison throughout, with each paragraph addressing a specific point of comparison across both films. For example, if comparing how each film represents conflict, one paragraph might examine how cinematography in Film A uses claustrophobic framing while Film B employs expansive wide shots, connecting these differences to distinct cultural contexts and cinematic traditions. Use comparative connectives such as 'similarly,' 'whereas' and 'in contrast' to make the dialogue between films explicit. Balance discussion of both films within each paragraph, giving roughly equal analytical attention to each.
