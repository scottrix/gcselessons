EC19: Fiscal Policy

Foundation Higher All Boards

Government spending, taxation, budget deficit/surplus, and national debt

Lesson Overview

Estimated Lessons: 3
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the key concepts and principles of fiscal policy
  • Apply knowledge of fiscal policy to real-world engineering contexts
  • Analyse and evaluate fiscal policy scenarios and data
  • Develop practical skills related to fiscal policy

Prerequisites

  • Basic understanding of economics concepts
  • Ability to follow safety procedures in practical settings

Materials & Equipment

  • Calculator for numerical calculations
  • Graph paper for drawing diagrams and charts
  • Case study materials (printed or digital)
  • Access to business/economics news sources
  • Spreadsheet software for data analysis (optional)
