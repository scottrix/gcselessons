MS5-1: Ownership and Control

Foundation Higher All Boards

Public service, commercial, community, state media, conglomerates, cross-media ownership

📋 Key Concepts

Overview: Public service, commercial, community, state media, conglomerates, cross-media ownership

Core Learning Points

  • Understand the fundamental principles and terminology
  • Apply knowledge to analyse examples and case studies
  • Evaluate effectiveness and impact in different contexts
  • Make connections to other areas of the specification

📝 Detailed Content

Key Point 1

Detailed explanation of the first major concept in this topic. This would cover definitions, principles, and theoretical frameworks relevant to ownership and control.

Key Point 2

Second major concept with detailed explanation, examples, and application to exam-style scenarios.

Key Point 3

Third concept focusing on analysis, evaluation, and synthesis of ideas. Includes real-world examples and case studies.

Key Point 4

Final concept covering advanced applications, synoptic links, and higher-tier extension material where applicable.

❓ Practice Questions

Q1: Explain the key principles of ownership and control.

Q2: Analyse how ownership and control applies to a specific media/film/design/food context.

Q3: Evaluate the effectiveness of different approaches in ownership and control.

Q4: Compare and contrast two examples of ownership and control in practice.

✅ Model Answers

  1. Key principles include [main concepts from the topic]. Students should reference specific terminology and theory.
  2. Application requires selecting relevant examples and applying theoretical frameworks to analyse them.
  3. Evaluation involves weighing strengths/limitations, considering context, and reaching a substantiated judgement.
  4. Comparison needs clear criteria, evidence from both examples, and a balanced conclusion.

🎯 Exam Tips

  • Learn key terminology and definitions precisely
  • Use specific examples from set products/texts
  • Structure answers: point, evidence, analysis, link back to question
  • For higher marks: evaluate, don't just describe
  • Make synoptic links across different strands

🧠 Problem-Solving Strategies

Approach: Break complex questions into component parts. Identify the command word. Select relevant theory. Apply to the specific context. Evaluate impact. Conclude with judgement.
Worked Example

When analysing ownership and control, first identify the relevant theoretical framework, then select appropriate evidence, apply the framework systematically, and evaluate the insights gained.

⚠️ Common Errors

Watch Out!

1. Wrong: Describing without analysing Correct: Always analyse - explain HOW and WHY, not just WHAT

2. Wrong: Using generic examples Correct: Use specific, detailed examples from set products or case studies

3. Wrong: Ignoring context Correct: Always consider historical, social, cultural, economic, political contexts

✍️ Extended Exam Question

Extended Response

6-9 marks: Analyse and evaluate the role of ownership and control in [specific context]. Refer to relevant theories and examples in your answer.

Model answer structure: Introduction with thesis → 3-4 developed paragraphs with theory/evidence/analysis → Evaluation of different perspectives → Conclusion with substantiated judgement.

Mark scheme: AO1 knowledge, AO2 application, AO3 analysis/evaluation

📊 AO3: Reason & Interpret

Reasoning and Interpretation

Consider a scenario involving ownership and control. Apply relevant theories to analyse the situation.

(a) Identify the key theoretical concepts relevant to this scenario.

(b) Apply these concepts to analyse the specific example.

(c) Evaluate the limitations of this theoretical approach.

Guidance: (a) Name theories and key terms. (b) Show step-by-step application with evidence. (c) Consider alternative interpretations, missing contexts, or theoretical critiques.
