Main Content (35 minutes)

Extended Answer

Extended question: Full-Mark Response Evaluate the impact of automation and Industry 4.0 on a medium-sized engineering manufacturer. Consider production efficiency, workforce and investment. [8 marks] <div class="

Automation and Industry 4.0 offer significant efficiency gains. Automated CNC machines and robots operate 24/7 with consistent quality, increasing output and reducing defect rates. IoT sensors enable predictive maintenance, reducing unplanned downtime by up to 50%. However, the workforce impact is significant: routine manual roles decline while demand for skilled technicians and programmers increases. Retraining existing staff is essential but costly. The investment required is substantial - robotic cells, IoT infrastructure and training can cost hundreds of thousands. For a medium-sized company, this must be prioritised - automating the most repetitive processes first, then expanding as ROI is demonstrated. Overall, long-term benefits in efficiency and competitiveness outweigh the costs, but the transition requires careful planning and workforce development.

Exam Tips: In technology questions, always consider both benefits AND drawbacks for balanced evaluation. | Name specific technologies (Industry 4.0, IoT, cobots) rather than general statements. | Link new technology to a specific engineering outcome: faster, cheaper, more precise or more flexible. | The social impact of automation (job displacement vs new skilled roles) is a common extended-response topic. | CAD/CAM integration is a reliable answer for how technology has changed manufacturing.
Common Errors: ✗ Automation will completely replace all human workers in engineering. ✓ Automation replaces routine manual tasks but creates demand for skilled workers in programming, maintenance and data analysis. ✗ Industry 4.0 means factories no longer need human oversight. ✓ Smart factories still require human oversight for strategy, maintenance and continuous improvement. ✗ Cobots can do everything traditional robots can do. ✓ Cobots are force-limited for safety, making them slower and less powerful than traditional robots. ✗ Digital twins are just 3D CAD models. ✓ A digital twin is a live, data-connected virtual model updated by real-time sensor data; a CAD model is static.
