Exam Tips: When discussing dance history, always link the period or figure to a specific professional work you have studied | Use precise dates and names — examiners reward accurate historical knowledge | Explain how historical developments influenced choreographic style, not just what happened | Compare at least two different periods or choreographers when asked about dance evolution | Remember that dance history questions often require you to analyse, not just describe
Common Errors: ✗ Modern dance and contemporary dance are the same thing. ✓ Modern dance refers to the early 20th-century movement (Graham, Cunningham); contemporary dance is a later blend of ballet, modern and postmodern styles. ✗ Ballet began in Russia. ✓ Ballet originated in the courts of Renaissance Italy and France; it later flourished in Russia during the classical period. ✗ Isadora Duncan founded contemporary dance. ✓ Duncan pioneered free expressive movement in the early 1900s, which was part of the modern dance movement; contemporary dance developed decades later. ✗ Dance history is just a list of dates and names. ✓ Dance history is about understanding how social, cultural and artistic changes shap
