Lesson 2: Core Concepts: choreographic intention &amp; communication

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on choreographic intention &amp; communication. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Key Fact: Choreographic intention is the purpose or message behind a dance — what the choreographer wants to communicate
Key Fact: Every choreographic decision should serve the intention, from movement choices to structure and staging
Key Fact: Mood is the atmosphere or feeling a dance creates — for example, tense, joyful, melancholic
Key Fact: Meaning is the deeper message or idea the dance conveys to the audience
Key Fact: Motifs are key movement phrases that represent the core idea of the dance
Key Fact: Developing and varying motifs throughout a dance reinforces the intention

Exam-Style Question

Exam Tips: Always state the choreographic intention clearly before analysing how it is communicated | Use specific movement examples to show how intention is conveyed — do not be vague | Remember that mood and meaning are different — mood is atmosphere, meaning is the deeper idea | In NEA tasks, write your intention as a clear statement and refer back to it throughout | For professional works, quote the choreographer's stated intention and link it to what you see
Common Errors: ✗ Choreographic intention is the same as the dance title. ✓ The title may hint at the intention, but the intention is the deeper purpose or message the choreographer wants to communicate through movement. ✗ Mood and meaning are the same thing. ✓ Mood is the atmosphere or emotional quality (e.g. tense, joyful); meaning is the deeper message or idea the dance conveys. ✗ If the audience does not understand the choreographer's intention, the dance has failed. ✓ Audience interpretation may differ from the choreographer's intention and this is valid — dance can communicate on many levels. ✗ A motif is just any movement in the dance. ✓ A motif is a specific, key movement phrase that represents the
