Main Content (35 minutes)

Health: A state of complete physical, mental and social wellbeing, not merely the absence of disease or infirmity (WHO definition). Health has three interconnected dimensions: physical, emotional/mental, and social.

Practice (10 minutes)

Q: Explain three physical health benefits of regular exercise.

Answer: Three physical health benefits: (1) Reduced risk of coronary heart disease - exercise strengthens the heart muscle, lowers blood pressure, increases HDL cholesterol and reduces fatty deposits in arteries. (2) Reduced risk of obesity - exercise burns calories and builds muscle mass, helping maintain a healthy energy balance and body weight. (3) Improved cardiovascular fitness - regular aerobic exercise increases VO₂ max, stroke volume and capillarisation, making the heart and lungs more efficient at delivering oxygen to working muscles.

Lesson 3: Application: physical, emotional and social health

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Quick Recall

Recall the key terms: Health. Define each in one sentence.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Parent/Teacher Guide: Let your student attempt each question alone first, then compare with the model answer. Award method marks for correct working even if the final answer is wrong.

Q1: Explain three physical health benefits of regular exercise.

Answer: Three physical health benefits: (1) Reduced risk of coronary heart disease - exercise strengthens the heart muscle, lowers blood pressure, increases HDL cholesterol and reduces fatty deposits in arteries. (2) Reduced risk of obesity - exercise burns calories and builds muscle mass, helping maintain a healthy energy balance and body weight. (3) Improved cardiovascular fitness - regular aerobic exercise increases VO₂ max, stroke volume and capillarisation, making the heart and lungs more efficient at delivering oxygen to working muscles.

Q2: How does exercise improve emotional and mental health? Refer to specific chemicals released during physical activity.

Answer: Exercise improves emotional and mental health through several chemical mechanisms: (1) Serotonin - exercise stimulates the release of serotonin, a neurotransmitter that improves mood and reduces depression. Low serotonin is linked to depression. (2) Endorphins - the body's natural painkillers are released during exercise, creating a feeling of euphoria (the "runner's high"). (3) Dopamine - exercise releases dopamine, which creates a sense of reward and motivation. Additionally, exercise reduces cortisol (stress hormone) levels, provides a sense of achievement, and improves self-esteem and body image - all contributing to better emotional health.

Q3: Describe three social health benefits of participating in sport and give a sporting example for each.

Answer: Three social health benefits: (1) Teamwork - sport requires working together towards shared goals, e.g. a football team coordinating attacks and defending as a unit. (2) Making friends - joining a club introduces you to people with shared interests, e.g. a new member of a running club meeting others at weekly sessions. (3) Sense of belonging - being part of a team gives identity and community, e.g. wearing a club kit and representing your local area creates a feeling of being valued and included.

Q4: Explain how regular exercise reduces the risk of type 2 diabetes.

Answer: Regular exercise reduces the risk of type 2 diabetes by: improving the body's sensitivity to insulin, meaning cells can more effectively absorb glucose from the blood; using glucose for energy during physical activity, which directly lowers blood sugar levels; helping maintain a healthy body weight, as obesity is a primary risk factor for type 2 diabetes; and building muscle mass, which increases the body's capacity to store and use glucose, keeping blood sugar levels more stable over time.

Q5: "Exercise is as effective as medication for treating mild depression." Evaluate this statement with reference to how exercise affects mental health.

Answer: This statement is supported by research showing that regular exercise can be as effective as antidepressant medication for mild to moderate depression. Exercise works through multiple pathways: serotonin release improves mood, endorphins reduce pain and create euphoria, dopamine provides reward and motivation, and the sense of achievement and routine combats the withdrawal and hopelessness of depression. Exercise also improves self-esteem, provides social interaction and reduces stress hormones. However, for severe depression, medication and professional therapy may still be necessary, and exercise should be part of a broader treatment plan rather than a complete replacement for medical care.

Q6: How can sport help combat social isolation? Explain with specific examples.

Answer: Sport combats social isolation by: providing regular, structured social contact through training sessions and matches, giving isolated people a reason to leave the house and interact with others; creating a sense of belonging through team membership and shared identity (e.g. wearing a club shirt, being part of a squad); introducing people to others with shared interests, making it easier to form friendships than in unstructured social settings; and providing a support network where members look out for each other's wellbeing. For example, an elderly person joining a walking group gains weekly social contact, friendship and a sense of community that combats the isolation they might otherwise experience living alone.

Lesson 4: Exam Practice: physical, emotional and social health

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Exam-Style Question

Attempt a past-paper style question on physical, emotional and social health from the exam board past paper finder (see Resources), then mark it against the scheme.

Exam Tips: Organise your answer into physical, emotional and social categories | Use specific chemical names: serotonin, endorphins, dopamine, cortisol | Physical health = disease reduction (CHD, obesity, diabetes) plus fitness improvements | Emotional health = mood, stress, self-esteem, emotional control, depression | Social health = teamwork, cooperation, friends, belonging - give sport examples | Remember the health triangle - all three dimensions are interconnected
Common Errors: Watch Out! Students often confuse emotional and social health. Wrong: "Making friends is an emotional benefit." Correct: "Making friends is a social benefit. Emotional benefits include stress relief, improved mood through serotonin release, increased self-esteem and reduced depression." Students often say exercise "makes you happy" without explaining the mechanism. Wrong: "Exercise makes you happy because it's fun." Correct: "Exercise improves mood by stimulating the release of serotonin and endorphins, which are neurotransmitters that reduce depression and create feelings of euphoria and wellbeing."
