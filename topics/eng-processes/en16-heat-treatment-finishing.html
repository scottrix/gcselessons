EN16: Heat Treatment and Surface Finishing

Foundation Higher All Boards

Annealing, hardening, tempering, case hardening, and coatings

Lesson Overview

Estimated Lessons: 3
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the key concepts and principles of heat treatment and surface finishing
  • Apply knowledge of heat treatment and surface finishing to real-world engineering contexts
  • Analyse and evaluate heat treatment and surface finishing scenarios and data
  • Develop practical skills related to heat treatment and surface finishing

Prerequisites

  • Basic understanding of engineering concepts
  • Ability to follow safety procedures in practical settings

Materials & Equipment

  • Workshop PPE: safety glasses, steel-toe boots, ear protection
  • Relevant tools and equipment for practical demonstrations
  • Material samples for handling and testing
  • Technical drawing equipment (drawing board, set squares, compass)
  • Measuring instruments (vernier calipers, micrometers, steel rules)
  • Access to workshop machinery (lathe, mill, drill press) as required
  • Risk assessment templates and COSHH sheets
