SPG3: Grammar and Syntax

FoundationHigherAll Boards

Write grammatically accurate sentences with correct word classes, tenses, and agreement.

Lesson Overview

Estimated Lessons: 2
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, Eduqas, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Apply spelling rules and patterns accurately
  • Use punctuation marks correctly and for effect
  • Construct grammatically accurate sentences
  • Write clear, well-organised paragraphs
  • Edit and proofread writing for accuracy

Prerequisites

  • Basic understanding of sentence structure
  • Familiarity with common spelling patterns

Materials & Equipment

  • Spelling rules reference sheets
  • Punctuation practice worksheets
  • Grammar exercises
  • Editing and proofreading checklists

Lesson 1: Word Classes and Tenses

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Sentence Surgery

Display a sentence with a grammar error. Fix it.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
  • Nouns: person, place, thing. Proper nouns take capitals.
  • Verbs: action or state. Must agree with subject.
  • Adjectives: describe nouns (the tall man / the man is tall).
  • Adverbs: describe verbs, often -ly (she ran quickly).
  • Tenses: past/present/future. Stay consistent unless deliberately shifting.
  • Common errors: 'me and him went' → 'he and I went'; 'she don't' → 'she doesn't'
Worked Example

Incorrect: 'Him and me was going to the shops when we seen our friend who was stood there.'

Correct: 'He and I were going to the shops when we saw our friend, who was standing there.'

Practice Task

Find and correct grammar errors in these 8 sentences.

Differentiation

Support:
Provide pronoun chart (I/me, he/him, etc.).
Stretch:
Rewrite sentences using different tenses.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Grammar Detective

Display paragraph with 5 errors. Find and correct them.

Assessment Criteria

  • Got it: Uses word classes accurately, consistent tense
  • Getting there: Mostly accurate but occasional errors
  • Not yet: Frequent grammar errors

Lesson 2: Sentence Structures and Syntax

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Sentence Combining

Give three short sentences. Combine into one complex sentence.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
  • Simple: one clause. 'The dog barked.'
  • Compound: two clauses + conjunction. 'The dog barked, but the cat ignored it.'
  • Complex: main + subordinate clause. 'Although the dog barked, the cat ignored it.'
  • Embedded clause: inserted into another. 'The dog, which had been barking, fell asleep.'
  • Syntax variation: 'Never had she felt so afraid' (inverted for emphasis).
Worked Example

Three simple: 'She arrived late. The meeting had started. She crept in.'

Combined: 'When she arrived late, the meeting had already started, so she crept in quietly.'

Practice Task

Rewrite simple sentences as complex sentences using subordinate clauses.

Differentiation

Support:
Provide subordinate clause word bank.
Stretch:
Write a paragraph using simple, compound, and complex sentences in sequence.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Sentence Variety Count

Students count sentence types in their writing.

Assessment Criteria

  • Got it: Uses range of sentence structures accurately
  • Getting there: Some variety but tends towards simple
  • Not yet: Sentences all simple or structurally flawed

Homework Suggestions

  • Complete a spelling or grammar worksheet on the topic covered
  • Practise proofreading a paragraph for errors
  • Write a short passage using the skills learned, then self-assess
