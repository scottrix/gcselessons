N3: Order of Operations

Foundation Higher All Boards

Apply the correct order of operations (BIDMAS/BODMAS) to evaluate numerical expressions involving brackets, powers, roots, and the four operations.

Lesson Overview

Estimated Lessons: 2
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, Eduqas, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • State and apply BIDMAS/BODMAS convention correctly
  • Evaluate expressions with brackets, indices, and four operations
  • Handle nested brackets and fraction lines as implied brackets
  • Insert brackets to make statements true
  • Use calculators correctly with order of operations

Prerequisites

  • Four operations with integers and decimals (N2)
  • Square numbers, cube numbers, square roots
  • Understanding of brackets as grouping symbols
  • Negative number arithmetic

Materials & Equipment

  • Scientific calculator
  • Mini whiteboards
  • BIDMAS poster/reference card
  • Expression cards for sorting

Lesson 1: BIDMAS Basics

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Different Answers?

Calculate: 4 + 3 × 2. Compare answers around the class. Why different?

Main Content (35 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
BIDMAS: Brackets, Indices, Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction.
Key Principle: Division and Multiplication have equal priority — work left to right. Same for Addition and Subtraction.

Brackets: ( ) [ ] { } — innermost first.
Fraction lines: Act as implied brackets — numerator and denominator evaluated separately.
Indices: Powers (², ³) and roots (√, ³√) before multiply/divide.
Worked Example 1: Basic BIDMAS

Evaluate: 12 - 4 × 2 + 6 ÷ 3

Step 1: Multiplication and division first (left to right)
4 × 2 = 8, 6 ÷ 3 = 2
Expression becomes: 12 - 8 + 2

Step 2: Addition and subtraction (left to right)
12 - 8 = 4, 4 + 2 = 6
Answer: 6

Worked Example 2: With Brackets and Indices

Evaluate: (5 + 3)² - 4 × (6 - 2)

Step 1: Brackets first
(5 + 3) = 8, (6 - 2) = 4
Expression: 8² - 4 × 4

Step 2: Indices
8² = 64
Expression: 64 - 4 × 4

Step 3: Multiplication
4 × 4 = 16
Expression: 64 - 16

Step 4: Subtraction
64 - 16 = 48
Answer: 48

Worked Example 3: Fraction Line as Brackets

Evaluate: (12 + 8) / (5 - 1)

Numerator: 12 + 8 = 20 (implied brackets)
Denominator: 5 - 1 = 4 (implied brackets)
Division: 20 ÷ 4 = 5
Answer: 5

BIDMAS Priority Order:
1. Brackets: ( ) [ ] { } — innermost first
2. Indices: x², x³, √x, ³√x
3. Division & Multiplication: left to right
4. Addition & Subtraction: left to right

Implied Brackets:
• Fraction line: (top) / (bottom)
• Root symbol: √(expression)
• Powers: (base)^(exponent)

Common Misconception

Multiplication always before division (or addition before subtraction)

Multiplication and division have EQUAL priority — work left to right. 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = 4 × 2 = 8, NOT 12 ÷ 6 = 2.

Differentiation

Support:
Colour-code steps (brackets=red, indices=blue, etc.). Use step-by-step templates. Start with 2-operation expressions.
Stretch:
Expressions with nested brackets: 3 × [4 + 2(5 - 1)]. Algebraic substitution: if x=3, evaluate 2x² + 5x - 1.

Plenary (5 minutes)

True or False

6 + 4 × 2 = 20 (F, it's 14)
(6 + 4) × 2 = 20 (T)
18 ÷ 3 × 2 = 3 (F, it's 12)
18 ÷ (3 × 2) = 3 (T)

Assessment Criteria

  • Got it: Consistently applies BIDMAS; handles nested brackets; uses fraction lines correctly
  • Getting there: Knows order but forgets left-to-right for equal priority; bracket errors
  • Not yet: Works strictly left to right; ignores brackets and indices

Lesson 2: Complex Expressions and Problem Solving

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Insert Brackets

Make true: 4 + 5 × 3 - 2 = 25 → (4 + 5) × (3 - 2) = 9 ✗
Try: 4 + 5 × (3 - 2) = 9 ✓
4 + (5 × 3 - 2) = 17 ✓

Main Content (35 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
Nested Brackets: Work from innermost out. Different bracket types help visibility.
Example: 2 × [3 + (4 × 2)] = 2 × [3 + 8] = 2 × 11 = 22

Calculator Use: Modern calculators follow BIDMAS. Use brackets for clarity.
For (4.5 + 2.3)²: type (4.5+2.3)x² = 46.24
For √(3² + 4²): type √(3x²+4x²) = 5

Negative Numbers in BIDMAS: -3² = -(3²) = -9. (-3)² = 9. The negative is not part of the base unless bracketed.
Worked Example: Nested Brackets and Negatives

Evaluate: 5 - 2[3 - (4 - 7)]

Step 1: Innermost bracket: (4 - 7) = -3
Expression: 5 - 2[3 - (-3)]

Step 2: Square brackets: 3 - (-3) = 3 + 3 = 6
Expression: 5 - 2[6]

Step 3: Multiplication: 2 × 6 = 12
Expression: 5 - 12

Step 4: Subtraction: 5 - 12 = -7
Answer: -7

Worked Example: Negative Squared

Evaluate: -4² + (-4)²

-4² = -(4²) = -16 (indices before negative)
(-4)² = (-4) × (-4) = 16 (brackets first)
Sum: -16 + 16 = 0
Answer: 0

Negative Numbers with Indices:
-x² = -(x²) — index applies first, then negative
(-x)² = (-x) × (-x) = x² — brackets first

Examples:
-5² = -25 (-5)² = 25
-2³ = -8 (-2)³ = -8
√(16) = 4 -√(16) = -4

Common Misconception

-3² = 9

-3² = -9. The index only applies to the 3, not the minus sign. For positive 9, write (-3)².

Differentiation

Support:
Explicitly write each step. Use different coloured pens for each BIDMAS stage. Calculator checking encouraged.
Stretch:
Create expressions equal to target numbers using given digits. Evaluate algebraic expressions with substitution. Work backwards: given answer, find missing number/bracket.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Challenge

Using digits 2, 3, 4, 5 once each and any operations, make 24.
(5 - 2) × 4 × 2 = 24? No, uses 2 twice.
(5 × 4) + (3 × 2) = 26
5 × 4 + 3 + 2 = 25
(5 + 3) × (4 - 2) = 16

Assessment Criteria

  • Got it: Evaluates complex nested expressions; inserts brackets correctly; handles negatives with indices
  • Getting there: Manages 2-3 step expressions; struggles with nested brackets or negative indices
  • Not yet: Ignores BIDMAS; cannot evaluate expressions with brackets

Homework Suggestions

  • BIDMAS worksheet with increasing complexity (no brackets → single brackets → nested)
  • "Insert brackets to make true" puzzle sheet
  • Calculator exercise: Evaluate 20 expressions, compare with manual working
  • CorbettMaths "Order of Operations" textbook exercise
