N13: Units and Conversions

Foundation Higher All Boards

Convert between metric units (length, mass, capacity). Convert metric to imperial approximations. Convert units of time, area, volume. Solve problems with compound measures (speed, density, pressure).

Lesson Overview

Estimated Lessons: 3
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, Eduqas, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Convert between metric units (mm, cm, m, km; g, kg, t; ml, l)
  • Convert metric to imperial using given approximations
  • Convert units of area (mm², cm², m², km², ha) and volume (mm³, cm³, m³)
  • Convert units of time and solve time problems
  • Work with compound measures: speed, density, pressure

Prerequisites

  • Place value and multiplying/dividing by 10, 100, 1000
  • Area and volume formulae (rectangle, triangle, cuboid, prism)
  • Understanding of compound measures (speed = distance/time)
  • Rearranging formulae (speed triangle)

Materials & Equipment

  • Measuring tools: rulers, tape measures, scales, measuring jugs
  • Conversion reference poster
  • Speed/density/pressure formula triangles
  • Stopwatches for practical speed/density

Lesson 1: Metric Conversions (Length, Mass, Capacity)

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Estimate and Measure

Estimate: height of door, mass of apple, capacity of mug. Then measure.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
Length: 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm
Multiply to go smaller (×10, ×100, ×1000). Divide to go larger.
2.5 km = 2500 m, 340 cm = 3.4 m, 0.07 m = 7 cm

Mass: 1 tonne (t) = 1000 kg, 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 g = 1000 mg
3.2 kg = 3200 g, 4500 g = 4.5 kg

Capacity: 1 l = 1000 ml = 100 cl, 1 cl = 10 ml
0.75 l = 750 ml, 3500 ml = 3.5 l

Method: Identify start and target units. Count steps. Multiply/divide by 10/100/1000 accordingly.
Worked Example 1: Length Conversions

Convert: (a) 4.3 km to m (b) 250 cm to m (c) 7.8 mm to cm (d) 0.005 km to cm

(a) 4.3 × 1000 = 4300 m
(b) 250 ÷ 100 = 2.5 m
(c) 7.8 ÷ 10 = 0.78 cm
(d) 0.005 km = 0.005 × 1000 = 5 m = 500 cm

Worked Example 2: Mass and Capacity

Convert: (a) 2.4 tonnes to kg (b) 350 g to kg (c) 0.6 l to ml (d) 2500 ml to l

(a) 2.4 × 1000 = 2400 kg
(b) 350 ÷ 1000 = 0.35 kg
(c) 0.6 × 1000 = 600 ml
(d) 2500 ÷ 1000 = 2.5 l

Metric Conversions (multiply/divide by powers of 10):
Length: km → m → cm → mm (×1000, ×100, ×10)
Mass: t → kg → g → mg (×1000 each step)
Capacity: l → cl → ml (×100, ×10) or l → ml (×1000)

Area: m² → cm² (×10000), cm² → mm² (×100)
Volume: m³ → cm³ (×1000000), cm³ → mm³ (×1000)
1 ha = 10000 m², 1 l = 1000 cm³

Common Misconception

1 m² = 100 cm²

1 m = 100 cm, so 1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10,000 cm². Area scale factor is squared.

Differentiation

Support:
Conversion ladder poster. "K H D U D C M" (kilo, hecto, deca, unit, deci, centi, milli). Practical measuring first.
Stretch:
Compound units: km/h to m/s. g/cm³ to kg/m³. Map scales (1:25000). Area/volume scale factors.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Quick Convert

3.7 km = 3700 m | 450 mm = 45 cm = 0.45 m | 2.5 t = 2500 kg | 0.03 l = 30 ml
1 m² = 10000 cm² | 1 cm³ = 1000 mm³ | 1 l = 1000 cm³

Assessment Criteria

  • Got it: Converts all metric units fluently; handles area/volume conversions; solves mixed problems
  • Getting there: Length/mass/capacity OK; area/volume conversions confused (×100 vs ×10000)
  • Not yet: Wrong direction (× instead of ÷); no concept of scale factor for area/volume

Lesson 2: Imperial, Time, and Compound Measures

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Imperial Estimates

1 inch ≈ 2.5 cm, 1 foot ≈ 30 cm, 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km, 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb, 1 pint ≈ 568 ml. Estimate: 6 ft in cm, 5 miles in km, 10 lb in kg.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
Metric-Imperial Approximations (given in exam):
1 inch ≈ 2.54 cm, 1 foot ≈ 30 cm, 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km
1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb, 1 lb ≈ 454 g
1 gallon ≈ 4.5 litres, 1 pint ≈ 568 ml

Time: 1 hour = 60 min, 1 min = 60 sec. NOT decimal!
3.5 hours = 3 hours 30 min (not 3h50m)
2h 45m = 2.75 hours = 165 minutes

Compound Measures: Speed = Distance/Time, Density = Mass/Volume, Pressure = Force/Area
Use formula triangles for rearrangement.
Worked Example 1: Metric-Imperial

Convert using approximations:
(a) 5 feet to cm: 5 × 30 = 150 cm
(b) 10 miles to km: 10 × 1.6 = 16 km
(c) 70 kg to lb: 70 × 2.2 = 154 lb
(d) 3 gallons to litres: 3 × 4.5 = 13.5 l

Worked Example 2: Time Conversions

(a) 4.25 hours to hours/minutes: 4h 15m (0.25×60=15)
(b) 3h 40m to hours: 3 + 40/60 = 3⅔ = 3.667 hours
(c) 150 minutes to hours: 150÷60 = 2.5 hours = 2h 30m
(d) 2.5 hours + 1h 45m = 4h 15m = 4.25 hours

Worked Example 3: Speed, Density, Pressure

Speed: Car travels 180 km in 2.5 hours. Speed = 180/2.5 = 72 km/h
Density: Block mass 4.5 kg, volume 0.002 m³. Density = 4.5/0.002 = 2250 kg/m³
Pressure: Force 500 N on area 0.25 m². Pressure = 500/0.25 = 2000 Pa (N/m²)

Compound Measures (Formula Triangles):
Speed = Distance / Time
Distance = Speed × Time
Time = Distance / Speed

Density = Mass / Volume
Mass = Density × Volume
Volume = Mass / Density

Pressure = Force / Area
Force = Pressure × Area
Area = Force / Pressure

Units: Speed: m/s, km/h, mph. Density: g/cm³, kg/m³. Pressure: Pa (N/m²)

Common Misconception

3.5 hours = 3 hours 50 minutes

0.5 hour = 30 minutes. 3.5 hours = 3h 30m. Decimal time ≠ hours/minutes.

Differentiation

Support:
Formula triangles. Time conversion table. "60 minutes in an hour" chant. Unit check: speed units = distance/time.
Stretch:
km/h to m/s: ×1000÷3600 = ÷3.6. Density conversions g/cm³ → kg/m³ (×1000). Pressure in different units.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Speed Check

108 km/h = 30 m/s (÷3.6)
20 m/s = 72 km/h (×3.6)
Density water = 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³
1 atmosphere ≈ 101,325 Pa

Assessment Criteria

  • Got it: Converts metric-imperial; handles time correctly; uses formula triangles for compound measures
  • Getting there: Metric-imperial with given conversions; time decimal errors; knows formulae but rearranges wrong
  • Not yet: No imperial approximations; 3.5h = 3h50m; cannot use formula triangle

Lesson 3: Area/Volume Conversions and Problem Solving

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Scale Factor

1 m = 100 cm. 1 m² = ? cm². 1 m³ = ? cm³. 1 cm = 10 mm. 1 cm² = ? mm². 1 cm³ = ? mm³.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Teaching Notes:
Area Conversions: Scale factor squared.
1 m = 100 cm → 1 m² = 100² = 10,000 cm²
1 cm = 10 mm → 1 cm² = 100 mm²
1 km = 1000 m → 1 km² = 1,000,000 m²
1 ha = 10,000 m² = 100 m × 100 m

Volume Conversions: Scale factor cubed.
1 m = 100 cm → 1 m³ = 100³ = 1,000,000 cm³
1 cm = 10 mm → 1 cm³ = 1000 mm³
1 l = 1000 cm³ = 1000 ml

Strategy: Convert all to same units BEFORE calculating area/volume.
Worked Example 1: Area/Volume Conversions

Convert: (a) 3.5 m² to cm² (b) 2500 cm² to m² (c) 2.4 m³ to cm³ (d) 500000 cm³ to m³

(a) 3.5 × 10000 = 35000 cm²
(b) 2500 ÷ 10000 = 0.25 m²
(c) 2.4 × 1000000 = 2,400,000 cm³
(d) 500000 ÷ 1000000 = 0.5 m³

Worked Example 2: Mixed Units Problem

Room: 4.2 m × 3.5 m. Carpet tiles: 50 cm × 50 cm. How many tiles?
Method 1: Room area = 4.2 × 3.5 = 14.7 m² = 147000 cm²
Tile area = 50 × 50 = 2500 cm²
Tiles = 147000 ÷ 2500 = 58.8 → 59 tiles (need whole tiles)
Method 2: Tiles along length: 420 ÷ 50 = 8.4 → 9 tiles
Along width: 350 ÷ 50 = 7 tiles
Total = 9 × 7 = 63 tiles (accounts for cutting)

Worked Example 3: Density with Unit Conversion

Gold density 19.3 g/cm³. Find mass of 5 cm³ gold bar in kg.
Mass = Density × Volume = 19.3 × 5 = 96.5 g
96.5 g = 0.0965 kg

Area/Volume Scale Factors:
Length: ×10 (cm→mm), ×100 (m→cm), ×1000 (km→m)
Area: ×100, ×10000, ×1000000 (square the length factor)
Volume: ×1000, ×1000000, ×1000000000 (cube the length factor)

Key Equivalences:
1 m² = 10,000 cm² = 1,000,000 mm²
1 m³ = 1,000,000 cm³ = 1,000,000,000 mm³
1 litre = 1000 cm³ = 1000 ml
1 ha = 10,000 m²

Common Misconception

Convert 2 m × 50 cm → 2 × 50 = 100 m²

Must convert to SAME units first: 200 cm × 50 cm = 10000 cm² = 1 m².

Differentiation

Support:
Conversion flowcharts. "Same units first" rule. Grid method for area: convert each dimension separately.
Stretch:
Scale factor problems: model 1:50, area scale 1:2500, volume 1:125000. Rates: flow rate (cm³/s → l/min). Pressure in different units.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Spot the Error

1 m² = 100 cm² ✗ (10,000)
1 cm³ = 10 mm³ ✗ (1000)
5 km² = 5000 m² ✗ (5,000,000)
2.5 l = 250 cm³ ✗ (2500 cm³)

Assessment Criteria

  • Got it: Converts area/volume correctly; solves mixed-unit problems; uses compound measures with conversions
  • Getting there: Length conversions OK; area/volume scale factor confusion; mixed unit problems
  • Not yet: No conversion for area/volume; calculates with mixed units; wrong scale factors

Homework Suggestions

  • Metric conversion worksheet (length, mass, capacity, area, volume)
  • Metric-imperial conversion problems (using given approximations)
  • Speed/density/pressure problems with unit conversions
  • Time calculation problems (timetables, journeys)
  • CorbettMaths "Metric Units", "Compound Measures", "Time" exercises
