LT19-religion-mythology: Religion, Mythology & Beliefs

All Boards Edexcel / OCR / Eduqas

State cult, household gods, festivals, mystery cults, mythology, afterlife, Christianity

Lesson Overview

Lesson: 2 of 4
Focus: Unseen translation technique and comprehension skills
Tier: Single Tier (Grades 1-9)
Duration: 50 minutes
Exam Boards: Edexcel, OCR, Eduqas

Learning Objectives

  • Master key vocabulary for religion, mythology & beliefs
  • Understand and apply unseen translation technique and comprehension skills
  • Develop vocabulary, grammar, translation skills in context
  • Build confidence with exam-style tasks
