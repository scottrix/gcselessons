Lesson 2: Core Concepts: practical observing skills

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on practical observing skills. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Key Fact: Dark adaptation takes 20–30 minutes; avoid white light — use red light to preserve night vision
Key Fact: Averted vision (looking slightly to the side) uses rod cells, which are more sensitive to faint light
Key Fact: Binoculars are excellent for beginners — 10×50 is a recommended specification
Key Fact: Always use a solar filter or projection method when observing the Sun; NEVER look directly at the Sun
Key Fact: Keeping an observation log records date, time, conditions, equipment and sketches
Key Fact: Telescope setup includes polar alignment, balancing, collimation (for reflectors) and finder scope alignment

Lesson 4: Exam Practice: practical observing skills

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Exam-Style Question

Attempt a past-paper style question on practical observing skills from the exam board past paper finder (see Resources), then mark it against the scheme.

Exam Tips: Solar safety is CRITICAL — both boards emphasise this; NEVER look at the Sun without proper filtration | 10×50 binoculars: 10× magnification, 50mm aperture — know this specification | Averted vision uses rods — this is examined on both specifications | Both AQA and Edexcel require practical observation as part of the course
Common Errors: ✗ Sunglasses are safe for solar observation ✓ Only certified solar filters (e.g. Baader film, welder's glass #14) are safe — sunglasses do not block enough IR/UV radiation ✗ Looking through the eyepiece briefly at the Sun is safe ✓ Even a fraction of a second of concentrated sunlight through a telescope can cause permanent eye damage or blindness ✗ Dark adaptation is instant ✓ Full dark adaptation takes 20–30 minutes; even a brief flash of white light resets the process
