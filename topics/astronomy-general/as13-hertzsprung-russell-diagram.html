Lesson 2: Core Concepts: the hertzsprung-russell diagram

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on the hertzsprung-russell diagram. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Key Fact: The HR diagram plots luminosity (vertical axis, increasing upward) against temperature/spectral class (horizontal axis, decreasing left to right)
Key Fact: The main sequence runs diagonally from top-left (hot, luminous) to bottom-right (cool, dim)
Key Fact: Red giants appear in the upper-right: cool but very luminous due to large size
Key Fact: White dwarfs appear in the lower-left: hot but dim due to small size
Key Fact: Supergiants span the top of the diagram across all spectral types
Key Fact: The instability strip (between main sequence and giants) contains variable stars like Cepheids

Lesson 4: Exam Practice: the hertzsprung-russell diagram

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Exam-Style Question

Attempt a past-paper style question on the hertzsprung-russell diagram from the exam board past paper finder (see Resources), then mark it against the scheme.

Exam Tips: Temperature axis goes BACKWARDS (right to left) — hot on left, cool on right — this catches many students out | Know the three main regions: main sequence, giant branch, white dwarf region | The Sun is on the main sequence — be able to mark its position | Both boards test HR diagram interpretation: be prepared to identify regions and describe what they represent
Common Errors: ✗ The temperature axis increases left to right like a normal graph ✓ The temperature axis DECREASES left to right — hot blue stars are on the left, cool red stars on the right ✗ Stars move along the main sequence over time ✓ Stars move OFF the main sequence as they evolve; they don't travel along it ✗ White dwarfs are cool because they are dim ✓ White dwarfs are actually very hot but dim because they have very small surface areas
