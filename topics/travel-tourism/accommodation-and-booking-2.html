FR39-accommodation: Accommodation & Booking

Foundation Higher All Boards

Accommodation vocab, imperative for requests, problems vocab, complaint letter

Lesson Overview

Lesson: 2 of 4
Focus: Developing comprehension strategies with authentic/semi-authentic texts
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, Eduqas, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Master key vocabulary for accommodation & booking
  • Understand and apply developing comprehension strategies with authentic/semi-authentic texts
  • Develop vocabulary, grammar, reading skills in context
  • Build confidence with exam-style tasks

Starter (5 min)

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter (5 min) (5 min)

Activity

Prediction task: from title/images, predict 5 words in text. Share predictions.

Main (35 min)

Duration: 50 minutes

Main (35 min) (35 min)

Activity

Reading: authentic/semi-authentic text on topic. Strategy: identify cognates, find verbs, use context. Questions: true/false, multiple choice, short answer in English/target language. Listening: authentic audio (announcement/interview/dialogue). Pre-teach key vocab. Play twice. Note-taking frame provided.

Plenary (10 min)

Duration: 50 minutes

Plenary (10 min) (10 min)

Activity

Compare reading vs listening: which was harder? Why? Note 3 new words learned.

Homework Suggestions

  • Re-read text, highlight unknown words, look up. Listen again at home, write summary in English.
  • Vocabulary learning: use spaced repetition (Anki/Quizlet/physical cards)
  • Grammar consolidation: complete additional practice exercises
  • Skill practice: listen to / read / write / speak using topic language
