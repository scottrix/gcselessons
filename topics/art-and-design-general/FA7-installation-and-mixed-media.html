installation and mixed media

FoundationHigherAll Boards

4 detailed 50-minute lessons with teaching scripts, worked examples, parent guides, and assessment criteria.

Lesson Overview

Total Lessons: 4
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson (200 minutes total)
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, Eduqas, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Explain the key ideas of installation and mixed media
  • Apply installation and mixed media to exam-style questions
  • Develop independent study skills
  • Build cross-topic connections

Prerequisites

  • Read the revision notes for installation and mixed media first (GCSE Revise summary)
  • Key vocab to pre-teach: Fine Art Exam Tips
  • Basic skills: reading the summary notes and answering the practice questions there

Materials & Equipment

  • Exercise book, coloured pens
  • Ruler
  • Printed revision notes (link below)
  • Internet for videos (see Resources)

Lesson 1: Introduction: installation and mixed media

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Quick Recall

Write down everything you already know about installation and mixed media. Then check against the key terms: Fine Art Exam Tips. Use a mini-whiteboard or paper.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Parent/Teacher Guide:
Before lesson: Read the script below. Pre-teach key vocab: Fine Art Exam Tips.
If stuck: Re-read the revision notes (link above), then break the content into smaller steps.
Extension: See the Stretch & Challenge ideas in Lesson 4.
Teaching Script (35 mins):
Mins 0-5 - Hook: "Today: installation and mixed media. By the end you will be able to answer exam questions on it unaided. It connects to the rest of Art and Design because the ideas here recur across the spec."
Mins 5-20 - Direct Instruction: Work through the core ideas below one at a time; after each, ask your student to explain it back in their own words.
Mins 20-30 - Guided Practice: Model the worked example together, then let your student attempt the first practice question with guidance.
Mins 30-35 - Independent Practice: 2-3 practice questions from Lesson 3 below, with immediate feedback.
First Look

Start with the revision notes summary, then attempt: Create a series of experimental studies exploring installation and mixed media. Annotate your process.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Check Out

Your student states one thing they learned and one question they still have about installation and mixed media.

Lesson 2: Core Concepts: installation and mixed media

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on installation and mixed media. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Key Fact: Installation transforms a whole space — the viewer physically enters the artwork, becoming part of the experience.
Key Fact: Mixed media combines different materials and processes in one piece — breaking down boundaries between art forms.
Key Fact: Found objects (ready-mades) bring existing meaning and context — Duchamp's Fountain challenged what counts as art.
Key Fact: Site-specific work responds to a particular location — its architecture, history, atmosphere or community.
Fine Art Exam Tips: Show the SPACE as part of the work — include floor plans, views from different positions. Document audience interaction if possible. Explain the CONCEPT clearly. Link to installation artists (Ai Weiwei, Christo, Kara Walker).

Practice (10 minutes)

Q: Create a series of experimental studies exploring installation and mixed media. Annotate your process.

Answer: A range of experimental studies showing different approaches to installation and mixed media, each annotated with media, technique and purpose. Show development from initial experiments to refined outcomes.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Explain Back

Your student teaches the key points back to you without looking. Fill any gaps immediately.

Lesson 3: Application: installation and mixed media

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Quick Recall

Recall the key terms: Fine Art Exam Tips. Define each in one sentence.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Parent/Teacher Guide: Let your student attempt each question alone first, then compare with the model answer. Award method marks for correct working even if the final answer is wrong.

Q1: Create a series of experimental studies exploring installation and mixed media. Annotate your process.

Answer: A range of experimental studies showing different approaches to installation and mixed media, each annotated with media, technique and purpose. Show development from initial experiments to refined outcomes.

Q2: Research an artist or designer who works with installation and mixed media. Write a critical analysis of their approach and explain how it has informed your own work.

Answer: A critical analysis naming a specific artist/designer, using specialist vocabulary, explaining their context and approach, and making clear connections to personal work.

Q3: Evaluate the effectiveness of your experiments with installation and mixed media. Which approaches were most successful and why?

Answer: A reflective evaluation identifying successful and less successful experiments, explaining WHY using technical and conceptual language, and showing how evaluation led to further refinement.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Error Review

Review any questions answered incorrectly. Identify whether the error was knowledge, method, or reading the question.

Lesson 4: Exam Practice: installation and mixed media

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Extended Answer

Extended question: Full-Mark Response Plan an installation or mixed media piece responding to a specific theme or site. Create a proposal including sketches, material lists, and a written rationale explaining your conceptual intentions. <div class="

A grade 9 response will: demonstrate strong conceptual thinking; explain how every material choice supports meaning; consider audience experience and interaction; reference installation artists; plan technical logistics; evaluate potential challenges. Mark scheme: AO1 (develop through investigation) + AO2 (refine through experimentation) + AO3 (record ideas and observations) + AO4 (present personal response) = total marks

Exam Tips: Address ALL four Assessment Objectives — AO1 (develop), AO2 (refine), AO3 (record), AO4 (present) | Show the FULL creative journey — from initial research to final outcome | Annotate your work using specialist vocabulary (not just "I like it") | Draw from primary sources wherever possible — real objects, not photos | Experiment with a RANGE of media and techniques before selecting | Make clear connections between artist research and your own developing work
Common Errors: Watch Out! Students often make mistakes with this topic. Wrong: Installation is just putting random objects in a room — it does not need skill. Correct: Successful installation requires careful CONCEPTUAL thinking, spatial awareness, and understanding of how viewers interact with space. Every element must be intentional — materials, placement, lighting, sound all contribute to meaning.
Stretch & Challenge (Grade 8-9):
  • Synoptic links: explain how installation and mixed media connects to another Art and Design topic you have studied
  • Real-world: research one real-world use or example of installation and mixed media
  • Critical: "What are the limitations of the models used in installation and mixed media?"

Plenary (5 minutes)

Assessment Criteria
  • Got it: Confident explanation + correct worked examples
  • Getting there: Main points OK, needs support with detail
  • Not yet: Confused on key concepts - re-run Lesson 2

Homework & Consolidation

  • Consolidation: Re-answer any Lesson 3 practice questions answered incorrectly (20 mins)
  • Retrieval: Write flashcards for the key terms: Fine Art Exam Tips (10 mins)
  • Exam practice: One past-paper question on installation and mixed media from the board websites (15 mins)
  • Extension: Explain installation and mixed media to someone else in your own words (10 mins)
