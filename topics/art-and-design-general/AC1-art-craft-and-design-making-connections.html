Lesson 2: Core Concepts: art craft and design making connections

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on art craft and design making connections. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Key Fact: Art explores ideas and personal expression; craft emphasises making, skill and material knowledge; design responds to needs, briefs and function.
Key Fact: The Art, Craft and Design title requires working in at least TWO different disciplines — this breadth is a STRENGTH, not a complication.
Key Fact: Cross-disciplinary approaches create richer work: painting informed by textile techniques; photography combined with printmaking; 3D design with graphic communication.
Key Fact: Your portfolio must show both BREADTH (multiple disciplines) and DEPTH (sustained development in each area), connected by a coherent theme.
Art, Craft and Design Exam Tips: Show SUSTAINED development in EACH discipline, not just one-off experiments. Explain CONNECTIONS between disciplines — how does your painting inform your photography, or your textile work influence your graphics? Create a COHERENT narrative across your portfolio.

Practice (10 minutes)

Q: Create a series of experimental studies exploring art craft and design making connections. Annotate your process.

Answer: A range of experimental studies showing different approaches to art craft and design making connections, each annotated with media, technique and purpose. Show development from initial experiments to refined outcomes.

Lesson 3: Application: art craft and design making connections

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Quick Recall

Main Content (35 minutes)

Parent/Teacher Guide: Let your student attempt each question alone first, then compare with the model answer. Award method marks for correct working even if the final answer is wrong.

Q1: Create a series of experimental studies exploring art craft and design making connections. Annotate your process.

Answer: A range of experimental studies showing different approaches to art craft and design making connections, each annotated with media, technique and purpose. Show development from initial experiments to refined outcomes.

Q2: Research an artist or designer who works with art craft and design making connections. Write a critical analysis of their approach and explain how it has informed your own work.

Answer: A critical analysis naming a specific artist/designer, using specialist vocabulary, explaining their context and approach, and making clear connections to personal work.

Q3: Evaluate the effectiveness of your experiments with art craft and design making connections. Which approaches were most successful and why?

Answer: A reflective evaluation identifying successful and less successful experiments, explaining WHY using technical and conceptual language, and showing how evaluation led to further refinement.

Lesson 4: Exam Practice: art craft and design making connections

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Extended Answer

Extended question: Full-Mark Response Plan a project that combines at least two different disciplines (e.g. fine art and photography, or textile design and graphic communication). Show how each discipline contributes to your concept and how they connect meaningfully. <div class="

A grade 9 response will: work confidently in at least two disciplines; show depth of development in each area; explain how the disciplines connect and inform each other; create a coherent portfolio linked by a strong theme; annotate with vocabulary from each discipline. Mark scheme: AO1 (develop through investigation) + AO2 (refine through experimentation) + AO3 (record ideas and observations) + AO4 (present personal response) = total marks

Exam Tips: Address ALL four Assessment Objectives — AO1 (develop), AO2 (refine), AO3 (record), AO4 (present) | Show the FULL creative journey — from initial research to final outcome | Annotate your work using specialist vocabulary (not just "I like it") | Draw from primary sources wherever possible — real objects, not photos | Experiment with a RANGE of media and techniques before selecting | Make clear connections between artist research and your own developing work
Common Errors: Watch Out! Students often make mistakes with this topic. Wrong: The Art, Craft and Design title is easier because you can do a bit of everything without going deep. Correct: The Art, Craft and Design title requires you to show both BREADTH across disciplines AND DEPTH within each — it is not a shortcut. You must demonstrate sustained development in at least two areas AND show how they connect meaningfully. This demands exceptional organisational and conceptual skills.
