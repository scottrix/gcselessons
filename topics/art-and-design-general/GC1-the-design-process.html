the design process

FoundationHigherAll Boards

4 detailed 50-minute lessons with teaching scripts, worked examples, parent guides, and assessment criteria.

Lesson Overview

Total Lessons: 4
Tier: Foundation and Higher
Duration: 50 minutes per lesson (200 minutes total)
Exam Boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, Eduqas, CCEA

Learning Objectives

  • Explain the key ideas of the design process
  • Apply the design process to exam-style questions
  • Develop independent study skills
  • Build cross-topic connections

Prerequisites

  • Read the revision notes for the design process first (GCSE Revise summary)
  • Key vocab to pre-teach: Graphic Communication Exam Tips
  • Basic skills: reading the summary notes and answering the practice questions there

Materials & Equipment

  • Exercise book, coloured pens
  • Ruler
  • Printed revision notes (link below)
  • Internet for videos (see Resources)

Lesson 1: Introduction: the design process

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Quick Recall

Write down everything you already know about the design process. Then check against the key terms: Graphic Communication Exam Tips. Use a mini-whiteboard or paper.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Parent/Teacher Guide:
Before lesson: Read the script below. Pre-teach key vocab: Graphic Communication Exam Tips.
If stuck: Re-read the revision notes (link above), then break the content into smaller steps.
Extension: See the Stretch & Challenge ideas in Lesson 4.
Teaching Script (35 mins):
Mins 0-5 - Hook: "Today: the design process. By the end you will be able to answer exam questions on it unaided. It connects to the rest of Art and Design because the ideas here recur across the spec."
Mins 5-20 - Direct Instruction: Work through the core ideas below one at a time; after each, ask your student to explain it back in their own words.
Mins 20-30 - Guided Practice: Model the worked example together, then let your student attempt the first practice question with guidance.
Mins 30-35 - Independent Practice: 2-3 practice questions from Lesson 3 below, with immediate feedback.
First Look

Start with the revision notes summary, then attempt: Create a series of experimental studies exploring the design process. Annotate your process.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Check Out

Your student states one thing they learned and one question they still have about the design process.

Lesson 2: Core Concepts: the design process

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on the design process. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Key Fact: A design brief defines the problem — who is the audience, what is the message, what are the constraints.
Key Fact: The design process is iterative: research, ideas, development, refinement, feedback, revision.
Key Fact: Mind-mapping and mood boards help generate and organise ideas visually at the start of a project.
Key Fact: Presenting to a client requires clear rationale — explain design decisions, not just show finished work.
Graphic Communication Exam Tips: Show the FULL JOURNEY — research, thumbnails, roughs, development, final. Annotate at every stage. Explain design DECISIONS — why this typeface, why this colour, why this layout? Show ITERATION.
TermMeaningExample
AnalysisUnderstand the problemRead brief, identify user needs, constraints
ResearchGather informationProduct analysis, user research, materials investigation
SpecificationDefine requirementsList measurable criteria the design must meet
IdeationGenerate ideasMind maps, thumbnails, SCAMPER, mood boards
DevelopmentRefine best ideaModelling, prototyping, testing, refining
EvaluationAssess against specTest against each specification point

Practice (10 minutes)

Q: Create a series of experimental studies exploring the design process. Annotate your process.

Answer: A range of experimental studies showing different approaches to the design process, each annotated with media, technique and purpose. Show development from initial experiments to refined outcomes.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Explain Back

Your student teaches the key points back to you without looking. Fill any gaps immediately.

Lesson 3: Application: the design process

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Quick Recall

Recall the key terms: Graphic Communication Exam Tips. Define each in one sentence.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Parent/Teacher Guide: Let your student attempt each question alone first, then compare with the model answer. Award method marks for correct working even if the final answer is wrong.

Q1: Create a series of experimental studies exploring the design process. Annotate your process.

Answer: A range of experimental studies showing different approaches to the design process, each annotated with media, technique and purpose. Show development from initial experiments to refined outcomes.

Q2: Research an artist or designer who works with the design process. Write a critical analysis of their approach and explain how it has informed your own work.

Answer: A critical analysis naming a specific artist/designer, using specialist vocabulary, explaining their context and approach, and making clear connections to personal work.

Q3: Evaluate the effectiveness of your experiments with the design process. Which approaches were most successful and why?

Answer: A reflective evaluation identifying successful and less successful experiments, explaining WHY using technical and conceptual language, and showing how evaluation led to further refinement.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Error Review

Review any questions answered incorrectly. Identify whether the error was knowledge, method, or reading the question.

Lesson 4: Exam Practice: the design process

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Extended Answer

Extended question: Full-Mark Response Respond to a design brief for a music festival poster. Show your complete design process from initial research and mood boards through idea generation to final design. Annotate each stage. <div class="

A grade 9 response will: demonstrate thorough research into the brief, audience and context; generate multiple ideas before selecting; show systematic development and refinement; annotate every decision with clear rationale; reference graphic designers and existing designs. Mark scheme: AO1 (develop through investigation) + AO2 (refine through experimentation) + AO3 (record ideas and observations) + AO4 (present personal response) = total marks

Exam Tips: Address ALL four Assessment Objectives — AO1 (develop), AO2 (refine), AO3 (record), AO4 (present) | Show the FULL creative journey — from initial research to final outcome | Annotate your work using specialist vocabulary (not just "I like it") | Draw from primary sources wherever possible — real objects, not photos | Experiment with a RANGE of media and techniques before selecting | Make clear connections between artist research and your own developing work
Common Errors: Watch Out! Students often make mistakes with this topic. Wrong: Start designing immediately — research wastes time. Correct: AQA AO1 requires you to DEVELOP ideas through INVESTIGATIONS informed by sources. Without research, your work lacks the contextual understanding that earns marks. Research into existing designs, target audience and context is ESSENTIAL.
Stretch & Challenge (Grade 8-9):
  • Synoptic links: explain how the design process connects to another Art and Design topic you have studied
  • Real-world: research one real-world use or example of the design process
  • Critical: "What are the limitations of the models used in the design process?"

Plenary (5 minutes)

Assessment Criteria
  • Got it: Confident explanation + correct worked examples
  • Getting there: Main points OK, needs support with detail
  • Not yet: Confused on key concepts - re-run Lesson 2

Homework & Consolidation

  • Consolidation: Re-answer any Lesson 3 practice questions answered incorrectly (20 mins)
  • Retrieval: Write flashcards for the key terms: Graphic Communication Exam Tips (10 mins)
  • Exam practice: One past-paper question on the design process from the board websites (15 mins)
  • Extension: Explain the design process to someone else in your own words (10 mins)
