Lesson 2: Core Concepts: the externally set assignment component 2

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Review Previous Lesson

Quick recap: write 3 key points from Lesson 1 on the externally set assignment component 2. Check them against the notes below.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Key Fact: The ESA gives you starting points from AQA — you choose one and develop it independently during the preparatory period.
Key Fact: The preparatory period is YOUR time to research, experiment and develop ideas — show the same depth as your portfolio.
Key Fact: The 10 hours supervised time is for producing your FINAL RESPONSE — all preparation must be complete before it starts.
Key Fact: Your ESA preparation must address ALL four AOs — examiners mark the preparation alongside the supervised work.
Art, Craft and Design Exam Tips: Start preparation IMMEDIATELY after receiving the paper — do not waste time. Address ALL FOUR AOs in your preparatory work. Plan your 10 hours carefully — create a schedule. Test materials and techniques BEFORE the supervised time. Your preparation folder is MARKED — make it as strong as your portfolio.

Practice (10 minutes)

Q: Create a series of experimental studies exploring the externally set assignment component 2. Annotate your process.

Answer: A range of experimental studies showing different approaches to the externally set assignment component 2, each annotated with media, technique and purpose. Show development from initial experiments to refined outcomes.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Lesson 3: Application: the externally set assignment component 2

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Quick Recall

Recall the key terms: Art, Craft and Design Exam Tips. Define each in one sentence.

Main Content (35 minutes)

Parent/Teacher Guide: Let your student attempt each question alone first, then compare with the model answer. Award method marks for correct working even if the final answer is wrong.

Q1: Create a series of experimental studies exploring the externally set assignment component 2. Annotate your process.

Answer: A range of experimental studies showing different approaches to the externally set assignment component 2, each annotated with media, technique and purpose. Show development from initial experiments to refined outcomes.

Q2: Research an artist or designer who works with the externally set assignment component 2. Write a critical analysis of their approach and explain how it has informed your own work.

Answer: A critical analysis naming a specific artist/designer, using specialist vocabulary, explaining their context and approach, and making clear connections to personal work.

Q3: Evaluate the effectiveness of your experiments with the externally set assignment component 2. Which approaches were most successful and why?

Answer: A reflective evaluation identifying successful and less successful experiments, explaining WHY using technical and conceptual language, and showing how evaluation led to further refinement.

Plenary (5 minutes)

Error Review

Review any questions answered incorrectly. Identify whether the error was knowledge, method, or reading the question.

Lesson 4: Exam Practice: the externally set assignment component 2

Duration: 50 minutes

Starter Activity (5 minutes)

Command Words

Review what these command words require: state (one point), describe (say what happens), explain (say why), compare (both sides), evaluate (judgement).

Main Content (35 minutes)

Extended Answer

Extended question: Full-Mark Response Choose one ESA starting point and plan your preparatory work. Create a timeline showing how you would address each AO during the preparatory period. Explain how you would use the 10 hours supervised time. <div class="

A grade 9 response will: show thorough, independent preparation addressing all four AOs; create a realistic, detailed timeline; demonstrate understanding that preparation is MARKED alongside the final piece; explain how the 10 hours would be used efficiently; plan for contingencies. Mark scheme: AO1 (develop through investigation) + AO2 (refine through experimentation) + AO3 (record ideas and observations) + AO4 (present personal response) = total marks

Exam Tips: Address ALL four Assessment Objectives — AO1 (develop), AO2 (refine), AO3 (record), AO4 (present) | Show the FULL creative journey — from initial research to final outcome | Annotate your work using specialist vocabulary (not just "I like it") | Draw from primary sources wherever possible — real objects, not photos | Experiment with a RANGE of media and techniques before selecting | Make clear connections between artist research and your own developing work
Common Errors: Watch Out! Students often make mistakes with this topic. Wrong: The ESA is an exam where you just make art for 10 hours with no preparation. Correct: The ESA has two parts: a PREPARATORY PERIOD (weeks of research, development and experimentation — like a mini-portfolio) and the 10 HOURS SUPERVISED TIME (where you create your final response). The preparatory work is marked alongside the final piece. Without thorough preparation, you cannot score well.
