📈 Economics

Foundation Higher All Boards

GCSE Economics develops understanding of how markets work, why they fail, and how governments intervene. Students study microeconomics (markets, producers, elasticity) and macroeconomics (economy, government policy, global economy). Data analysis, calculations, and case studies build quantitative and evaluative skills.

📊 Course Overview

GCSE Economics develops understanding of how markets work, why they fail, and how governments intervene. Students study microeconomics (markets, producers, elasticity) and macroeconomics (economy, government policy, global economy). Data analysis, calculations, and case studies build quantitative and evaluative skills.
